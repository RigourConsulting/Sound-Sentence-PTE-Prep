Candidate Guide & Installation

Sound & Sentence | PTE Prep is packaged as a standalone, self-contained Android application. It requires no account creation, no external server connectivity, and no subscription activation.

Android Compatibility

Supports Android 8.0 (Oreo, API 26) through Android 15 (API 35). Optimized for mainstream modern devices including the Samsung Galaxy A56, Google Pixel, and Xiaomi devices with responsive scaling across phone and tablet form factors.

Local APK Installation

For candidate preview builds and institutional trials, install via Android Package Installer (`.apk`) or via Android Debug Bridge (`adb install -r sound-sentence-debug.apk`). The package size is ~25 MB with all 36 authentic audio tracks bundled.

Offline Preparedness

Once installed, the application runs 100% offline. You can practice in airplane mode, during commutes, or in study environments without internet access.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Why does the app need microphone permissions?

The Android RECORD_AUDIO permission is required exclusively for speaking practice drills (Read Aloud, Repeat Sentence, Describe Image, Re-tell Lecture, and Answer Short Question) and for the speaking sections of the Form 1 Mock Exam. The app records your spoken response locally so you can immediately listen back to your take, compare your acoustic rhythm and pacing against the reference audio, and assess yourself against official PTE rubrics. Your voice recordings never leave your device.

2. How do I enable or fix microphone recording issues?

If audio recording fails to start:

  1. Open Android Settings on your phone.
  2. Go to Apps → Sound & Sentence → Permissions.
  3. Select Microphone and choose "Allow only while using the app".
  4. Ensure no background audio recording apps (such as screen recorders or call recorders) are holding an exclusive microphone lock.

3. Why doesn't the app provide an automated "AI Score" for speaking?

Most commercial apps claiming to provide "instant AI pronunciation scores" pass candidate audio through generic third-party speech recognition engines that produce volatile, uncalibrated scores that bear no statistical correlation to Pearson's actual scoring engine.

Sound & Sentence | PTE Prep adheres to psychometric honesty: we provide verifiable acoustic reference benchmarks, detailed criteria rubrics (Content, Pronunciation, Oral Fluency), and instant audio playback. Objective tasks (Reading, Writing, Listening) are evaluated deterministically with complete precision.

4. Why does exam audio only play once?

To preserve authentic examination simulation fidelity, official PTE Academic examination audio tracks cannot be paused, rewound, or replayed. In Form 1 Mock Exam mode, the single-play constraint is strictly enforced. In catalogue drill mode, candidates may replay audio tracks to facilitate deliberate ear training and pronunciation refinement.

5. How does the Writing scoring work?

For Summarize Written Text and Write Essay, the app checks:

  • Form Compliance: Exact word-count thresholds (e.g. 5–75 words for summary in a single sentence; 200–300 words for essay).
  • Sentence Structure: Verification of single full-stop requirement for summary tasks.
  • Grammar & Spelling: Pure Kotlin dictionary verification detecting misspelled lexis and punctuation anomalies.

6. What are "Denominator-Isolated Analytics"?

Standard apps often compute overall accuracy across all possible questions, which falsely depresses your score if you haven't completed every drill. Our denominator-isolated analytics calculate your Objective Accuracy, Form Compliance, and Spoken Takes strictly against tasks you have attempted, providing an authentic picture of your current competence.

7. How do I reset my practice records or clear storage?

To clear your drill records or reset completed mock exams:

  • Go to the in-app Settings & Privacy tab and tap "Reset Practice Records".
  • Alternatively, go to Android Settings → Apps → Sound & Sentence → Storage → Clear Storage.
