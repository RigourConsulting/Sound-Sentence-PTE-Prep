Candidate Testing & Installation

During active preview and device evaluation, approved candidate testers can install the application directly onto compatible Android devices.

1. Device Compatibility

Requires Android 8.0 (API 26) or higher. Tested and verified on modern Android devices, including the Samsung Galaxy A56 and Pixel phones.

2. Self-Contained Content

The package (~21 MB) includes all 36 audio tracks and 22 task packs (20 core PTE Academic + 2 additional speaking formats). Practice drills and mock exams work offline after installation.

3. Installation

Testers install the verified Android package (`.apk`) via the standard Android Package Installer or via Android Debug Bridge (`adb install -r sound-sentence.apk`).

Frequently asked questions

Why does the app require microphone access?

Microphone permission is required exclusively for speaking practice exercises (Read Aloud, Repeat Sentence, Describe Image, Re-tell Lecture and Answer Short Question) and the Speaking section of Mock Form 1. The app records your voice locally so you can play it back immediately and compare your rhythm and pronunciation with the reference audio. Your voice recordings never leave your device.

How do I resolve microphone recording issues?

If recording does not start:

  1. Open Android Settings on your phone.
  2. Tap Apps → Sound & Sentence → Permissions.
  3. Select Microphone and ensure it is set to "Allow only while using the app".
  4. Verify that no other audio recording or calling app is holding an exclusive microphone lock in the background.

Why can exam audio only be played once?

In an official PTE Academic examination, audio stimuli play only once without pause or rewind. Mock Form 1 enforces this constraint to simulate real examination pressure. In the Task Catalogue drill mode, candidates can replay reference audio freely to support deliberate practice and ear training.

How does Writing evaluation work?

Writing exercises are evaluated for form compliance (such as word count ranges: 5–75 words for summaries; 200–300 words for essays), sentence structure (ensuring summaries contain exactly one full stop), and vocabulary accuracy. We do not provide generic or uncalibrated holistic scores that could mislead candidates about their exam readiness.

How can I reset my practice history or clear recordings?

You have complete control over your data:

  • Go to the Settings tab in the app and tap "Purge Local Audio Takes" to delete all voice recordings.
  • Tap "Reset Practice Database" to clear all question attempt history and reset metrics.
