Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 14 November 2026 • Entity: Rigour Consulting Australia • Package ID: au.com.rigourconsulting.soundandsentence • Contact: rigour.consulting@gmail.com • Version: 2.2

Our Privacy Principle: On-Device Practice Storage

Sound & Sentence | PTE Prep is designed to keep your study activity private on your handset. Your spoken audio recordings, written essay responses, test answers and score records are stored locally in app-private storage on your Android device. We do not operate candidate tracking servers, collect personal analytics telemetry or sell user data.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Rigour Consulting Australia ("we", "our") handles information in connection with the Sound & Sentence | PTE Prep Android application (package identifier au.com.rigourconsulting.soundandsentence, "the Application") and this website.

2. What data the app handles

The Application processes information entirely within the local Android operating system sandbox:

Data Category Description & Purpose Storage Location & Handling
Spoken Audio Recordings Candidate voice takes for speaking practice drills and mock examinations. Stored locally in app-private storage (context.filesDir/recordings). Audio is never streamed or uploaded to any remote server.
Written Text Responses Candidate summaries and essay drafts. Stored locally in app-private storage (context.filesDir).
Practice & Score History Item attempt timestamps, objective answers, score indices and task status. Saved atomically to local JSON files (practice_history.json) with backup journal protection.
App Preferences Theme preferences, audio volume levels and onboarding completion states. Stored in local Android preferences on the device.

3. Microphone permission (RECORD_AUDIO)

The Application requests the Android RECORD_AUDIO permission strictly to allow you to record your speaking answers.

  • Audio is recorded only when you start a speaking drill or begin a speaking item in the mock examination.
  • Recordings are saved locally as AAC/M4A audio files for immediate playback and benchmark review.
  • Audio recordings are never transmitted across any network interface.
  • You may revoke microphone permission at any time through your Android device settings without affecting writing, reading or listening exercises.

4. Zero third-party trackers or telemetry

Unlike many educational mobile apps, Sound & Sentence | PTE Prep contains:

  • No Analytics SDKs: No Google Analytics for Firebase, Mixpanel, Amplitude or Flurry.
  • No Crash Telemetry SDKs: No external services capturing stack traces or device identifiers.
  • No Advertising Networks: Zero ads, banner trackers or advertising ID collectors.
  • No Mandatory Accounts: No sign-in with Google, Facebook or email required to use the application.

5. Google Drive backup exclusions

To prevent inadvertent cloud synchronization of candidate voice recordings, the Application includes custom Android backup descriptors (backup_rules.xml and data_extraction_rules.xml) that explicitly exclude all audio recording directories and session journals from automated Google Drive device backups.

6. Data retention and deletion controls

Because all records reside locally on your phone, you maintain complete control:

  • Purge Audio Takes: Tap "Purge Local Audio Takes" in the in-app Settings screen to delete all recorded voice files instantly.
  • Reset Practice Database: Tap "Reset Practice Database" in Settings to clear all attempt records and reset scores to zero.
  • Device Settings: Clear app storage via Android Settings → Apps → Sound & Sentence → Storage → Clear Storage.
  • Uninstall: Removing the app permanently removes all application-private data from your device.

7. Google Play services, external links & website hosting

While all practice drills, audio recordings and mock examination history remain on your device:

  • Google Play Distribution & Billing: The Application is distributed via Google Play and integrates the Google Play Billing Library for optional preparation pass previews and entitlement management. When you install the app or interact with Google Play billing controls, Google Play processes transaction and account metadata under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • External Links: Opening the Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, or email support links from the in-app Settings screen launches your device's browser or email client.
  • Website Hosting & Fonts: This public website is hosted via GitHub Pages. Standard web server access logs (such as IP addresses and browser user-agents) may be processed by GitHub Inc. in accordance with GitHub's Privacy Statement to maintain service security and availability. Web fonts are served via Google Fonts; no tracking cookies or advertising scripts are deployed by Rigour Consulting Australia.
