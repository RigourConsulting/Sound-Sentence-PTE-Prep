Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 9 November 2026 • Entity: Rigour Consulting Australia • Contact: rigour.consulting@gmail.com • Version: 2.0 (Brand Release)

Core Privacy Commitment: Local-First Data Sovereignty

Sound & Sentence | PTE Prep is engineered on a strict local-first architecture. Your voice recordings, written essays, test responses, diagnostic scores, and practice history are stored exclusively on your physical device. We operate no user tracking servers, collect no telemetry, and harvest no personal data.

1. About This Policy & Publisher Identity

This Privacy Policy explains how Rigour Consulting Australia ("we", "us", "our") manages information in connection with the Sound & Sentence | PTE Prep mobile application ("the Application"). We are committed to protecting candidate privacy in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) set out in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and applicable international data sovereignty standards.

2. Categories of Data Handled

The Application processes the following categories of data strictly within the local Android operating system sandbox:

Data Category Examples Storage Location & Scope
Microphone Audio Recordings Spoken takes for Read Aloud, Repeat Sentence, Describe Image, Re-tell Lecture, Short Questions. Local private cache (context.cacheDir). Never transmitted across network interfaces.
Written Text Responses Candidate summaries and essays for Summarize Written Text and Write Essay. Local private internal storage (context.filesDir).
Diagnostic Scores & History Form 1 examination results, objective task accuracy, rubrics, and drill timestamps. Atomic JSON persistence files on local device storage.
Application Settings Audio volume preferences, dark mode selection, and onboarding acknowledgements. Local Android DataStore / SharedPreferences.

3. Microphone Permission (RECORD_AUDIO)

The Application requests the Android RECORD_AUDIO runtime permission solely to enable candidates to capture their spoken responses for immediate playback and benchmark comparison during speaking practice drills and mock examinations.

  • Audio capture occurs only when the candidate explicitly initiates a recording countdown or presses the record button.
  • Microphone input is encoded locally in uncompressed PCM or compressed AAC format.
  • Audio is never streamed to external speech-to-text APIs or remote evaluation services.
  • Candidates may revoke the microphone permission at any time in Android System Settings without affecting non-speaking tasks.

4. Zero Third-Party Telemetry & Trackers

Unlike typical educational and commercial mobile software, Sound & Sentence | PTE Prep is built without third-party tracking components:

  • No Analytics SDKs: No Google Analytics for Firebase, Mixpanel, Amplitude, or Flurry.
  • No Crash Reporting SDKs: No Firebase Crashlytics or Sentry sending stack traces to third parties.
  • No Advertising Networks: Zero ads, banner trackers, or advertising IDs (AAID/IDFA).
  • No Account Creation: No sign-in with Google, Facebook, or email required.

5. Cloud Backup Exclusions

To prevent inadvertent cloud synchronization of candidate voice recordings and test sessions, the Application implements custom Android backup descriptors (res/xml/backup_rules.xml and res/xml/data_extraction_rules.xml). All audio caches and session journals are explicitly excluded from Google Drive automated device backups.

6. Data Retention & User Control

Because all records reside locally on your device, you have complete and exclusive control over your data:

  • In-App Reset: You can clear practice history or individual drill takes from the in-app Settings screen.
  • Operating System Deletion: Navigating to Android Settings → Apps → Sound & Sentence → Storage → Clear Storage immediately and permanently destroys all recordings and scores.
  • Uninstalling the App: Removing the Application permanently deletes all application-private storage.

7. Children's Privacy

The Application is designed for adult candidates and students preparing for the academic English examination. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or store personal information from children under the age of 16.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be published on this website with an updated Effective Date. Because the Application operates entirely offline, updates to policy terms will also accompany new application release builds.
